What the signal score means

Local model

Each trade gets an observed-trade anomaly score from a small local model: how unusual is this trade versus the same wallet's prior history? The model reads behavioral and contextual signals only — trading pace, the gap since the last trade, how focused the wallet is on this market, how extreme the entry price is, and the wallet's past rate of outsized trades.

  • It is an anomaly score, not a profit prediction. A high score means "worth a look," not "this trade will win."
  • It is honest about its limits. The model deliberately excludes the trade's own size/value (which the training label is derived from), so its reported accuracy is a realistic estimate of skill rather than an inflated one. See Train Model for the current metrics and the precision/recall table.
  • Flags are rare by design. The operating threshold favors a few high-confidence flags over flagging everything, so most trades stay unmarked.
  • Analyze any trade with AI. Use the Analyze with AI link on All Trades, a wallet's trades, or a dashboard event — it runs the analysis on the spot. The write-up factors in the wallet's real resolved-market track record (win rate and ROI), so it reflects actual outcomes, not just stored value.
  • The outcome model is separate research. It stays experimental unless it beats market-implied probability on held-out resolved markets. Even then, it is a research signal, not a guaranteed trading system.
  • Alerts are monitoring, not automation. Telegram can notify you about large or unusual activity, but the app does not place trades or promise a profitable action.

Reading resolved-market performance

Real PnL

On a wallet's profile (and the leaderboard) you'll see realized PnL, ROI, win rate, and markets won/lost. These are computed only from markets that have actually resolved — positions are valued at settlement, so the numbers are real outcomes, not estimates.

  • Realized PnL / ROI nets your buys and sells per market and adds the settlement payout of whatever you still held when it resolved. ROI is measured against the cash you put in.
  • Win rate & markets won/lost count each resolved market once, by whether your net position there made or lost money.
  • "Not enough resolved market data yet" means this wallet has no resolved markets recorded — refresh it (or run the resolution backfill) and the real numbers appear as markets settle. We never show a fabricated figure.

Summary

The whole tutorial in one place

{{ app_name or "PolySignal" }} is a review tool, not a trading bot — it watches wallets and helps you read their activity, but it never places trades.

  • Workflow: add wallets → refresh to pull trades → scan the dashboard → filter All Trades → check Sync when something looks off.
  • Signal score: a local model's anomaly score (how unusual a trade is for that wallet), not a profit prediction. Flags are rare by design; open Why flagged? on a trade for the details.

  • AI analysis: the Analyze with AI link runs on demand and is grounded in the wallet's real resolved-market track record.
  • Alerts: Telegram can notify you about large or unusual trades. It is monitoring only — the app never trades or promises a profitable action.
