What the score means. The model estimates how unusual an observed trade is versus the wallet's own prior history, from behavioral and contextual signals only (trading pace, timing gaps, market focus, price extremity, the wallet's past outlier rate). It is an anomaly score, not a profit forecast, and it is not calibrated to a probability — treat a high score as "worth a look", not a guarantee. Reported metrics are point estimates on a single temporal split and will move as more data arrives.
